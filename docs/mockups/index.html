Design sketches for specs 0001 and 0002, not the real app. Visuals here are simplified browser animations standing in for the Pi's shaders; band and song names are placeholders.

1. Projector output with overlay

Three overlay templates on three modes. Any template works over any mode.

2. Control UI (phone)

Opened from any phone or laptop on the venue Wi-Fi.

3. XR18 mixer

Engineer view, channel detail, and a musician's own monitor page.

4. Fonts

Proposed bundled set. All are free to use, modify and redistribute (SIL Open Font Licence), so they can ship inside the public repo. You can also upload any TTF or OTF you have the rights to, such as your logo typeface.
